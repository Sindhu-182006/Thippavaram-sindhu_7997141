Community Bulletin

The Local Community Event Portal continues to bring residents together through cultural celebrations, environmental awareness programs, sports competitions, and educational workshops. Citizens are encouraged to participate actively in upcoming events to strengthen community relationships and improve local engagement. This year’s special events include music festivals, food carnivals, charity marathons, art exhibitions, and tree plantation drives. Residents can register online and receive instant updates regarding schedules and venue information. The city council aims to provide better digital accessibility and improve public interaction through this community platform.
